Hello! Thank you for participating in my game!

This game is a demonstration of my Javascript, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JQUERY knowledge. This game was created for ICS-128 Winter 2025 semester as part of the IMD Program. For my final project i wanted to choose the "choose your own adventure" catagory because I like making adventures and thought it would be in interesting challenge.

I wanted to make a basic adventure that highlights as many elements of what I have learned in to one experience, with a focus on creating "combat encounters". While the current functionality of these encounters is currently limited, I would like to expand on them in the future. Limitations of how long I had and the amount of work it took to get what I do have working meant that the experience can feel rather one-dimentional for now, but I hoped that what I did add is cool and fun!
